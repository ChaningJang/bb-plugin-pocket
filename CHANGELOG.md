# Pocket changelog

Changes to Pocket's pages and server. They reach you as soon as your bb reloads the plugin: no app install needed.
Newest first.

## 0.4.17
- About & what's new: see which Pocket and app version you're on, what's built but not installed yet, and what's next.

## 0.4.16
- Notifications screen: choose approvals, questions and "your turn"; quiet hours (silent delivery); your registered
  phones; a harmless test notification.

## 0.4.15
- A question's Reply… in a notification answers it only when the question takes typed answers; otherwise it's sent
  as a message.

## 0.4.14
- Walk shows "BB is talking" only while BB is actually audible, with no flicker mid-reply.

## 0.4.13
- A haptic when the long-press menu opens.
- Sheets really do swipe down to close.

## 0.4.11 – 0.4.12
- Swipe from the left edge to go back (in the iPhone app).

## 0.4.10
- In the iPhone app, Walk runs natively (keeps going with the screen locked) and haptics are real.

## 0.4.9
- Walk lock screen: a dim screen that ignores pocket touches; hold to unlock.

## 0.4.8
- Change an existing thread's access (Auto / Full access) from the model chip.

## 0.4.7
- Choose Auto or Full access when starting a thread.
- New Artifacts icon.

## 0.4.6
- Attach files and photos with the paperclip.

## 0.4.4 – 0.4.5
- Thread mentions become tappable chips; file links in messages open in Pocket.
